# Story bible: The L-Team (adventure id `stay`)
A second, shorter adventure on the same engine: a caper. Names are `{tokens}` from `cast.toml` (members `m1`..`m8`,
codenames `stay_*` in `[terms]`); this document uses the default cast. Read `docs/story-bible.md` and `docs/voice.md`
first: the eight sound the same here. Romance stays chaste.

## Premise
Early 2027. {m2} (Rae) has started his military service at **Camp Pinecone**, a fictional training camp in the
pines (no real unit, base, insignia or rank structure). A letter arrives at the dorm with a rabbit sticker on the
flap: the food is beige, the sergeant has a whistle, he misses the cats, and he misses... the sentence stops.
{m1} (Sol) reads it nine times. {m3} (Dae) reads it once and gets out the whiteboard.

When a rescue needs more nerve than sense, you call **{stay_team}**: seven idols in plush animal onesies,
a yellow delivery van, and a plan that is mostly vibes.

**The twist (decided):** they pull off the whole elaborate breakout, and on the hill at dawn the camp's Major,
out of breath, hands {m2} the weekend leave pass that started at 06:00. He could have walked out the front gate. The
reunion happens anyway. He's back by Sunday 18:00 and the Major asks for a selfie with the quokka.

## Tone rules for this adventure
- Cartoon caper: nobody is hurt. Fights are slapstick (honks, whistles, soup), defeated enemies "need a lie-down".
- Military service is treated with respect: {m2} is a good soldier, the camp is kind underneath, the joke is on
  the crew's over-planning, never on serving. No real weapons; the "tank" is a soup trolley.
- The caper homage lives in tropes: the planner with a whiteboard, the muscle who will not fly, the smooth face,
  the unhinged pilot, the welding montage, the van through the fence, and the planner's catchphrase
  (`{stay_catch}`). Never quote the show; its title appears only in the credits' apology list.
- Mascot costumes are generic plush animal onesies: wolf, quokka, rabbit, ferret, pig, chick, puppy, fox.

## Chapters (about 10 minutes each)
**1. The Letter** (Dae, Sol, Lio) — the dorm and the street. Sol reads the letter. Dae recruits: Lio (baking, refuses
any plan with a helicopter in it), Ari (mirror), Jun (reading the fine print: "this is a terrible idea, I'm in"),
Nico (mid-game, "legally"). Minigame: timing bar, jump-start the van. Fights: Feral Fancams on the street.
Trope cutscene **the crew reveal** (`stay_crew`): seven onesies in a row in front of the van.

**2. The Build** (Dae, Bo, Lio, Sol) — a scrapyard. Bo welds banana-yellow armour onto the van (grid trace minigame:
weld along the seams of a rabbit shape). Boss: **Scrap Hound 3000**, the yard's robot guard dog (Jun objects on
Kevin's behalf). The plan board: a choice scene where kind answers raise the meter. Trope: **the montage**
(`stay_montage`).

**3. The Gate** (Ari, Nico, Jun, Sol) — outside Camp Pinecone. Ari talks past the guardhouse (dialogue choices),
Nico crawls the vents (grid path minigame), Jun files a false report about a goose, and the goose turns out to be
real: boss **Commander Honk**. Trope **the van through the fence** (`stay_fence`): straight into the kimchi pots.

**4. Inside the Camp** (Rae alone, then the crew) — Rae's side. Morning drill: rhythm minigame, march in step. Rae
finds rabbit-onesie footprints and a note in Sol's handwriting in his boot. The crew bursts in; the boss is
**Sergeant Whistle**, who blows the whistle as an attack. Trope **the laugh** (`stay_laugh`): Rae sees seven
plush animals in the mess hall and laughs out loud, for the first time since he enlisted.

**5. The Getaway** (Dae, Lio, Jun, Rae; then everyone) — the motor pool and the hill. Lio refuses the
helicopter, drinks warm banana milk and falls asleep on his own, and they carry him aboard. Minigame: timing bar,
Jun's take-off. Boss: **Major Bunker** in the soup-trolley "tank". Trope **the flight** (`stay_flight`). On the hill
at dawn: the leave pass. Ending A (meter 20+): **the reunion** (`stay_reunion`), Sol and Rae share earphones while
the sun comes up. Ending B: **the gate** (`stay_gate`): the whole camp watching, the Sergeant blows the whistle and
everyone claps. Epilogue: back by Sunday 18:00. Credits.
