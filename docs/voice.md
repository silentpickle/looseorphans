# Voice guide

Read this before writing any dialogue. The bible says what happens; this says how it sounds.

## Tone
- A K-drama that knows it's a K-drama. Earnest feelings, ridiculous framing. Never mean about the members.
- Lines are short: one or two sentences each, three nodes per NPC talk, five to nine for a scripted scene.
- One joke per node at most. Let sincere beats land clean (no joke on the line after a confession).
- Parody names for every drama character; real stage names for the eight. Never a lyric.
- Romance is chaste: wrist grabs, umbrellas, notes, "hyung". The near-kiss is always interrupted.
- Choices (`choices`) are tone, not puzzles: both answers continue; the kinder one gives `meter: 2`, the funnier one
  `meter: 1`. Never a wrong answer.

## The eight
- **Sol**: fast, spiralling, self-interrupting ("I'm fine! Totally fine!"). Capitals when panicking. Calls Rae
  "Raemin-hyung". Writes the Quokka Notes: short, brave on paper, signed "-S".
- **Rae**: dry, deadpan, three words where ten would do ("...What."). Cats as deflection. Tsundere: the care
  shows in actions, never in words, until the finale.
- **Dae**: leader voice, "You okay, guys?", gentle dad energy, slips into Aussie slang when tired. Takes the
  hit for everyone. The one who can DECLARE.
- **Ari**: theatrical, aesthetic, narrates the lighting. Breaks the fourth wall once per chapter at most.
- **Bo**: gym metaphors, loud confidence, secretly the softest. "Don't think. Lift."
- **Lio**: sunshine, "good morning" at any hour, brownies as love language, the deep voice is a weapon.
- **Jun**: roasts with love, precise, the only one reading the fine print. Puppy ears, never mentions them.
- **Nico**: maknae privilege, innocent face, gets away with everything, calls it "legally".

## Drama cameos
- Each drama's cast speaks in its genre: Ongsang in small-town warmth and full-volume cops; Squish Game in
  flat announcer menace; Goryeo in court formality broken by Sol's modern slang; The Glory in weather-report
  cadence; the bog in soft folk riddles; Hamilsung in rhymed couplets (badly); KPDH in idol-interview polish.
- Director Gwi reads clauses. Everything he says is a contract term.

## Running gags (one touch per chapter)
- Sol and Rae almost meet. A note, a silhouette, a reflection, a letter.
- Someone says "LOOSE ENDS knows."
- Lio is baking, wherever they are.
- A Quokka Note (`note_N` flag, `meter: 2`) hidden in each chapter, two in the finale.
